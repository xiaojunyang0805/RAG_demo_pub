# Motor M-80 — datasheet notes

| Item | Datasheet value |
|---|---|
| Rated voltage | 48 V |
| Rated current | 12 A continuous |
| Peak current | 30 A for 3 s (datasheet); our TR-3 stall measured 31.2 A |
| Pole pairs | 4 |
| Encoder | absolute, 12 bit (4096 counts/rev), RS-485 or SPI output (factory option) |
| Winding limit | 130 °C (class B); the vendor recommends a 120 °C cut-off |
